Project duration: 2026.7.27 ~ 2026.10.2

Research question: How the influences of HOMA2-IR (Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance) on uACR (Urine Albumin‑to‑Creatinine Ratio) are mediated by proteins

**Covariates used in the analysis**
1. Age as of recruitment
2. Gender
3. Ethnicity
4. Diabetes duration (age as of recruitment – age of onset years)
5. Bmi
6. Systolic blood pressure
7. Hba1c value
8. Angiotensin converting enzyme (Ace) inhibitor
9. Sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (Sglt2) inhibitor
10. estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR)
11. Angiotensin receptor blocker (Arb)

Level 1 screen proteins **individually**
Level 2 screen proteins **collectively**

This is my independent project done during internship at KTPH. Raw clinical data (mody_yt2d_230726-olink.xlsx) was provided by clinical research unit, MODY team in KTPH.

More detailed information about the mediation analysis can refer to the Mediation analysis.pdf
